honeycow

this is not the cow you are looking for.

You reached this page because something on your network resolved a hostname whose DNS answer pointed at our IP, then connected to it over HTTP. Most often that means a scanner or misbehaving client queried this DNS server expecting a real authoritative response.

Honeycow is a deliberately incorrect DNS server — an NS squatter that synthesizes plausible-looking answers for any name in any zone. We do not own the domains we reply about. The IP your client connected to is ours; the records that pointed it here are fabricated.

If you operate a zone that honeycow is squatting on

Email abuse@honeycow.net from a contact tied to the zone you want exempted, and we will add it to the block list. Future queries for that zone (and its subnames) will return REFUSED instead of synthesized records.

Source: github.com/mclose/honeycow. Sibling project: chaoscow, the polite cousin.
