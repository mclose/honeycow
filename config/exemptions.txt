# --- Public internet-research scanners -------------------------------------
# We REFUSE for these scanner-research zones rather than bluff-answer.
# The queries are still fully logged (handler=exempt, response_kind=REFUSED)
# and surfaced in the morning report's "research scanners" section. The
# rationale is yin/yang: honeycow is the passive listener, these orgs are
# the active scanners, and we don't want to falsely validate amplifier paths
# in their open-resolver / open-service reports.
